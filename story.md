## Intro
 
### intro-prologue
 
You are a young detective, standing in the bitter cold North Pole.
 
Who would have thought that your search to prove once and for all that Santa does exist has led you to find Santa's workshop, but after all that hard work to get here, you have also found out that he has gone missing.
 
It's December 24th, Christmas Eve, and no one has heard from Santa in over a month. Mrs Claus is beside herself with worry, and their son is talking about stepping in and putting on his father's red suit.
 
Your task now is to find Santa, rescue Christmas, and finally get that promotion that you deserve.
 
But the trail has gone cold, snow cold as they say here...
