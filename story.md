# Story
 
## Intro
 
### intro-prologue
 
You are a young detective, standing in the bitter cold North Pole.
 
Who would have thought that your search to prove once and for all that Santa does exist has led you to find Santa's workshop, but after all that hard work to get here, you have also found out that he has gone missing.
 
It's December 24th, Christmas Eve, and no one has heard from Santa in over a month. Mrs Claus is beside herself with worry, and their son is talking about stepping in and putting on his father's red suit.
 
Your task now is to find Santa, rescue Christmas, and finally get that promotion that you deserve.
 
But the trail has gone cold, snow cold as they say here...

## Phone
 
### phone-call-santa
 
You dial the number you found after hacking into the Interpol database.
 
It rings. And rings...
 
Until you hear, *"Ho ho ho! Merry Christmas! Please leave a message after the bell."*
 
Voicemail. Obviously it wouldn't be that easy.
 
- [Sigh dramatically and hang up](#phone-sigh)
### phone-sigh
 
Frustrated, you try again, and again you hear: *"Ho ho ho! Merry Christmas! Please leave a message after the bell."*

## Elf

### elf-approach

You walk towards a smaller wooden building. The smell of fresh hay and reindeer hangs in the air, mingling with the thick smoke from a makeshift fire burning in an old metal drum. A handful of elves gather around it, holding their hands over the flames.

One catches your eye. They grimace at the sight of you, then turn away and go back to warming their hands.

What do you do?

- [Warm your hands by the fire](#elf-warm-hands)
- [Ask about Santa](#elf-ask-last-seen)
- [Threaten them](#elf-threaten)

### elf-warm-hands

You silently walk up to the metal drum. The only sound is the fire crackling and the snow crunching underneath your boots.

You decide not to immediately start interrogating the elf. Instead, you stand by the fire and wait.

- [Wait](#elf-wait-by-fire)

### elf-wait-by-fire

You stand there in silence, holding your hands out to the flame.

You notice the other elves start to drift away, leaving you alone with the elf who made eye contact earlier.

In a gruff voice, they say, *"I could do with a cigarette."*

- [Offer a cigarette](#elf-offer-cigarette)
- [Apologise — you don't have one](#elf-apologise)

### elf-offer-cigarette

The elf takes a long drag and coughs.

*"Thanks. I needed that."*

You stay beside the fire for a little longer. The tension between you seems to ease.

- [Ask about Santa](#elf-ask-about-santa)

### elf-apologise

You shake your head.

*"Sorry. I don't have one."*

The elf shrugs, unimpressed, and goes quiet again, watching the flames.

You've clearly failed to make much of an impression.

- [Ask about Santa anyway](#elf-ask-about-santa-cold)

### elf-ask-about-santa

*"So what do you know about Santa's disappearance?"*

The elf takes another drag and looks towards the workshop.

For the first time, they seem willing to talk.

- [Listen](#elf-listen)

### elf-listen

A few minutes of silence pass before the elf looks up, stares at you, and sighs.

*"It's been a long festive period. Demand's at a record high, the big man's nowhere to be seen, and everyone's pretending everything's fine."*

He takes another drag.

You get the feeling that, for once, someone here might actually tell you something useful.

- [Ask about his family](#elf-ask-about-family)
- [Ask about the workers](#elf-ask-about-workers)

### elf-ask-about-family

*"I heard he's been having trouble at home. Arguments. Lots of them. That's all I can say."*

He flicks the cigarette butt into the snow and slowly turns away.

You've finally got something to work with.

- [Ask about the workers](#elf-ask-about-workers)

### elf-ask-about-workers

*"Look kid, if you're looking for answers, you might want to speak to the reindeer handlers."*

You raise an eyebrow.

*"The stables?"*

The elf nods.

*"They're the ones who'd know if something's been... off."*

With that, he turns and walks back towards the workshop, leaving you standing beside the dying fire.

The stables suddenly seem a lot more interesting.

- [Head to the stables](#stables-arrive)

### elf-ask-last-seen

*"So, when's the last time you saw Santa?"*

The elf eyes you warily, clearly annoyed by the direct question.

They don't answer.

For a moment, neither of you says anything.

You realise you've probably come on a little too strong.

- [Back off and wait by the fire](#elf-wait-by-fire)
- [Push them for an answer](#elf-ask-about-santa-cold)

### elf-ask-about-santa-cold

You start asking them questions about Santa's disappearance — when they last saw him, when they last spoke to him, whether they'd noticed anything unusual.

The elf just shrugs and stares into the fire.

*"Look, I'm just a line elf. I make toys. I don't see nothin'. People like me don't exactly get invited to the important meetings."*

They're clearly not going to give you anything voluntarily.

- [Threaten them](#elf-threaten)
### elf-threaten

You lean closer, lowering your voice.

*"Look, I'm asking nicely. Now tell me the last time you saw Santa."*

The elf stares back at you, completely unimpressed.

*"Or what?"*

You hold their gaze.

You've pushed them too far now. There probably isn't any going back.

- [Punch them in the face](#elf-roll-punch)

### elf-roll-punch

You clench your fist.

This is a terrible idea.

You take a swing.

**Roll a D20.**

- [1-9: You miss](#elf-miss-punch)
- [10-19: You hit the elf](#elf-hit-punch)
- [20: Critical hit](#elf-crit-punch)

### elf-miss-punch

Your fist sails past the elf's head.

They duck far quicker than you expected, leaving you stumbling forward slightly.

The elf looks at you.

For a moment, neither of you says anything.

You try to recover from the embarrassment.

- [Try to play it off](#elf-intimidate)

### elf-hit-punch

Your fist connects with the elf's face.

They stumble backwards, clutching their jaw.

You immediately realise you've probably made this situation considerably worse.

The elf slowly looks back at you.

- [Try to play it off](#elf-intimidate)

### elf-crit-punch

You catch the elf clean on the chin.

They stumble backwards and hit the ground with a thud.

For a moment, they don't move.

You stare down at them, wondering exactly how badly you've just messed up.

The elf groans.

- [Try to play it off](#elf-intimidate)

### elf-intimidate

You straighten yourself up and try to act as though punching an elf was completely intentional.

The elf stares at you, clearly deciding whether you're worth the trouble.

Eventually, they sigh.

*"Fine. What do you want to know?"*

You've got another chance to ask your questions.

- [Ask about Santa](#elf-ask-about-santa)
